# TREATMENT — 《阿北的牙不痛了》 (Abei's tooth doesn't hurt anymore)

Style: 1930s Rubber Hose Cartoon (`rubber-hose`). Language on screen: Traditional Chinese (Taiwan). No voice; period title cards + hot-band score + foley.
Format: vertical 1080×1920 (Reels), 24 fps, 30 s, tempo 120 bpm (1 beat = 12 frames = 0.5 s, 15 bars of 4/4). Loudness −14 LUFS.
Gate: the style's 4:3 film gate turned upright (3:4, 1080×1440, rounded corners, soft inner shadow), centred on the 1080×1920 canvas; the black bars above and below stay black (they keep the Reels UI off the picture).

## 1. Candidate structures

A. **Inside / outside intercut.** Outside: Abei strolls through town, numb and delighted, eating everything. Inside his mouth: the bad tooth throws a jazz party that grows with every bite. The wear-off cuts the music dead; the party screams; a spring fires Abei back to the chair.
B. **The chase.** The dental chair grows wheels and hunts Abei through the city; instruments sing along. (Fun, but one idea: chase. No turn.)
C. **The tooth leaves.** The numb tooth hops out and tours the world, postcards arrive. (Most surreal, hardest to read at thumbnail size; no band stop.)

**Chosen: A.** It has one subject (the numb tooth that thinks the problem is gone), one goal (keep partying), one turn (the band stops), and the medium's own strength: the whole world dances, then freezes. The cliché for this style is a chase (B); A uses the band stop as the joke.

## 2. Logline and arc

Abei decides his tooth is cured because it went numb, and walks out; inside his mouth the bad tooth and the cavity bugs throw a jazz party that gets wilder with every sweet he eats, until the anaesthetic wears off and the band stops dead.

- Setup (bars 1–4): the syringe sings, Abei sits up, "牙不痛了，不弄了"; the dentist stretches after him; he walks out.
- Turn (bars 5–10): iris into the mouth, the party builds; title card "三小時後"; hard stop, freeze, silence; the first sound is a tiny "ouch".
- Ending (bars 11–15): all the teeth scream, Abei is fired back by a spring and lands in the chair; the dentist snaps his gloves on, the bugs queue with tiny suitcases; "麻藥不是治好，只是暫時不痛"; Abei: "……再打一次"; iris closes on the singing syringe (loops to the opening).

## 3. Benchmark

- Early Fleischer shorts (c. 1930–33, e.g. *Swing You Sinners!*) and *Cuphead*'s finish.
- Learn: the whole world dances in sync (walls included), surreal transformation, a band stop as a gag, an iris used as a camera and as a prop, a title card rolling up like a window shade.
- Don't take: Bimbo / Betty or any Fleischer / Cuphead character, melody, shot or font; the meme panda and the photo in the reference post.

## 4. Cast (all original)

- **阿北**: a round uncle, flat cap, thick moustache, suspenders, huge shoes. Numb cheek: crooked grin, a drool bubble.
- **醫師**: a tall thin dentist, head mirror, mask pulled under the chin when talking, white gloves with a snap. Pie eyes.
- **Syringe** (singer), **the bad molar** (the conductor, a hole in its side), **three small teeth** (top hats, Charleston), **cavity bugs** (caterpillar band: trumpet with a plunger mute, tuba, washboard).
- Town props: ice-cream cone, lollipop, soda bottle (all alive, all breathing on the beat).

## 6. Beat sheet

0 s syringe sings the jingle → 2 s Abei sits up (stinger) → 3 s card "牙不痛了，不弄了" → 4 s dentist stretches (slide whistle up) → 6 s iris → 8 s party starts (band enters) → 12 s Abei eats (xylophone stings) → 14 s peak, key change → 16 s window-shade card "三小時後" → 17 s band STOP, freeze → 19.5 s tiny "哎唷" → 20.5 s screams, orchestra hit → 22 s spring (boing, slide whistle) → 24 s chair catches (cymbal) → 26 s card, gloves snap → 28 s syringe jingle returns, iris.

## 7. Cue map

Tempo 120 bpm, 12 frames per beat, bar = 2 s. Theme: one short syncopated 4-bar tune in C, restated: solo syringe voice (bars 1–2, kazoo + piano) → full band in the mouth (bars 5–8: stride piano, trumpet with plunger mute, trombone smears, tuba oom-pah, banjo on 2 & 4, washboard) → key change up a tone at bar 7 → STOP on bar 9 beat 2 → bar 10 silence (only 24 Hz projector clatter) → bars 11–12 chaos (trombone raspberry, slide whistle) → bars 13–14 sparse (piano + woodblock) → bar 15 syringe jingle returns.
Every cut, card and key action lands on a beat.

## 8. Sound design

| Section | Bed | Foley | Music |

| 5–7 (8–16 s) | optical hiss | tooth taps (porcelain: hard inharmonic), bug footsteps (woodblock), sweets "chomp" | full band |
| 8–10 (16–20 s) | **only the 24 Hz gate clatter** | one tiny "哎唷" | **hard stop** |
| 11–14 (20–28 s) | hiss | spring boing, window crash (glass + bell), glove snap | stings, then sparse piano |
| 15 (28–30 s) | hiss | needle pop | jingle |

Two real silences (bar 10 and the gap before the glove snap); the first sound after the first silence is the "哎唷". Two transitions with sound over the cut (the band bleeds into the window-shade card; the spring's whistle runs over the cut into the clinic).

## 9. Subtitles and titles
